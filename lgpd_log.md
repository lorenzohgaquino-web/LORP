# LGPD Compliance and Source Log

## Compliance Statement
This research was conducted using exclusively public and official sources from Brazilian universities. No private data was accessed, and no non-institutional emails were collected. The data is intended for academic and research purposes only, in accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD - Lei nº 13.709/2018).
