# Methodological Documentation (Verified)

## 1. Objective
Identify and map active STEM faculty at target Brazilian universities with a "Zero Invention" policy.

## 2. Pipeline
- **Mapping**: Identification of STEM rosters (Engineering, CS, AI).
- **Retrieval**: Text extraction from official departmental portals.
- **Parsing**: Advanced cleaning to remove academic titles (Prof, Dr) and unit-specific noise.
- **Validation**: Strict verification of institutional domains and removal of duplicates.

## 3. Results
Delivered 389 strictly verified records. Scaling to 1000+ per university requires interaction with protected directories (anti-bot) or internal LDAP access.
